Documents

SWGDE encourages stakeholder participation in the preparation of documents. Suggestions for modifications are welcome and must adhere to all requirements as stated in our Disclaimer and Redistribution policies, which are attached to the front of every document. Electronic submissions for Request for Modifications may be completed online here.
SWGDE also encourages the use and redistribution of our documents. The Disclaimer and Redistribution policies (also included in the cover pages to each document) also establish what is considered SWGDE’s Intellectual Property. The policies for the Use and Redistribution of our information and documents is also defined therein. Electronic submission of a Notice of Use/Redistribution may be completed online here.
